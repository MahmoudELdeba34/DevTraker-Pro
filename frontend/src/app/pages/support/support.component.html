Common questions

How do I start tracking time on a task?

Open a project from the sidebar, click a task card, and use the timer widget — or pick a task from the top bar timer menu.

I was invited but cannot sign in

Use the setup link your admin sent you, or ask them to resend credentials from the Members page.

Who can add me to a workspace?

Workspace admins and HR can onboard employees or add existing teammates from the Members page.
